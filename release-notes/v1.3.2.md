## Music Selector 1.3.2: context-aware soundtrack switching

- Fix: picking **Cryptid OST** (or switching to it mid-shop) played the base Balatro music in the shop, ordinary blinds, packs and boss blinds, because Cryptid only has tracks for special situations. The Cryptid menu theme of the current gameset now plays in those situations, and the Jimball, Code, Exotic and Big tracks still take over when their trigger is active. Set `ordinary_music = "balatro"` at the top of `assets/sounds/cryptid/mpack_cryptid.lua` for the old behaviour.
- Fix: **Going in Blind** had no Celestial pack track (track 3 copied the Arcana rule). Track 2 is now Arcana and other packs, track 3 is Celestial.
- Fix: every soundtrack checked `booster_pack.removed` (lowercase), which never matches, so pack music could keep playing after a pack closed. It now uses `REMOVED` like the base game.
- Fix: **Alternative Soundtrack** shop, Arcana and Celestial tracks now stop once the shop or pack is removed.

Install: extract the zip into your Mods folder (files are at the zip root, put them in a folder named `Balatro-Music-Selector-Fixes-and-Features`), or update through the Mod Updater.
